How it works

Computers cannot read feelings directly. Here is the 4-step journey every food review takes:

🧹
Step 1

Clean the text

We remove messy HTML tags, punctuation, and extra spaces so only the meaningful words remain.

🔢
Step 2

Turn words into numbers

Important words like "delicious" or "stale" get counted and converted into math scores (TF-IDF).

🗳️
Step 3

Models vote

6 different machine learning models study the numbers and decide if the review sounds happy or unhappy.

🎯
Step 4

Show the result

We show you the final answer, how confident the models were, and which words swayed the decision.

Class split: 84% happy vs. 16% unhappy.
Because 84% of customers were already happy, a lazy model guessing "happy" every single time would still be right 84% of the time! That is why we evaluate models with ranking scores (ROC-AUC out of 100) instead of raw accuracy.
