How raw text flows through our architecture

From 568,454 Stanford SNAP raw reviews to calibrated ensemble predictions:

📦
Stage 1

Raw Dataset

Ingests 568,454 authentic customer reviews spanning 10+ years across 74,258 gourmet food products.

🧹
Stage 2

Preprocessing

Deduplication removes 174k cross-variant reviews, strips HTML, preserves negations, and stems tokens.

🔢
Stage 3

Feature Extraction

Converts cleaned tokens into 10,000 sublinear TF-IDF n-grams and CountVectorizer Bag-of-Words matrices.

👑
Stage 4

Ensemble Verdict

All 6 models calculate smooth probabilities; the mean consensus P_ens = 1/6 ∑ P_i yields our final prediction.
